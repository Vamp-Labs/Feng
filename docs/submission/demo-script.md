# FENG — Demo Video Script

**Duration:** 2:00
**Core Message:** Turn your investment thesis into something others can discover and follow.

## Creative Direction

**Opening hook:**
"What if your next investment didn't start with a stock ticker—but with an idea?"

**Story arc:**
Thesis → Strategy → Discovery → Follow → Participate → Onchain Proof

**Tone:** Confident, provocative, product-first. Avoid sounding like a technical walkthrough.

---

## 0:00–0:12 — The Hook

**Screen:** Start with the Explore page. Briefly highlight trending strategies, then focus on AI Will Win.

**Narration:**

"What if your next investment didn't start with a stock ticker—but with an idea? What if you could turn your conviction about the future into a strategy others could discover and follow? Meet Feng."

**On-screen text:**

YOUR THESIS.
YOUR STRATEGY.
YOUR COMMUNITY.

---

## 0:12–0:30 — Discover the Thesis

**Screen:** Show the Explore page, open AI Will Win, and highlight the thesis and stock allocations.

**Narration:**

"Imagine you believe AI will define the next decade. Today, that idea might stay in a tweet or a group chat. On Feng, it becomes AI Will Win: a transparent strategy built around NVIDIA, AMD, and other AI leaders. The thesis, the creator, and the allocation—all in one place."

**On-screen text:**

FROM AN IDEA
TO A DISCOVERABLE STRATEGY.

---

## 0:30–0:43 — Follow the Idea

**Screen:** Click Follow. Show the updated button and follower count.

**Narration:**

"Found a thesis you believe in? Follow it. Your conviction doesn't have to stay private—and discovering an investment idea doesn't have to mean researching every stock from scratch."

**On-screen text:**

DISCOVER. FOLLOW. STAY CONNECTED.

---

## 0:43–1:08 — Participate

**Screen:** Open the participation panel. Enter 1,000 test USDG, highlight the testnet label, and confirm the transaction.

**Narration:**

"And when you're ready, Feng lets you participate through one unified strategy experience. I'll put in one thousand test USDG. This is a testnet demo, not real money. I confirm the transaction, and Feng records my participation in the strategy."

**On-screen text:**

ONE THESIS.
ONE STRATEGY.
ONE SIMPLE EXPERIENCE.

---

## 1:08–1:28 — Prove It Onchain

**Screen:** Show the confirmed transaction on the Robinhood Chain testnet explorer, then open the Portfolio page.

**Narration:**

"But this isn't just a pretty interface. Here's the confirmed transaction onchain. And back in my portfolio, I can see my AI Will Win position. The idea is simple: discover a strategy, participate, and track it—all through Feng."

**On-screen text:**

REAL TESTNET TRANSACTION.
VERIFIABLE ONCHAIN.

---

## 1:28–1:43 — The Creator Economy

**Screen:** Open @alex's creator profile and show the published strategies.

**Narration:**

"And it works on the creator side, too. Anyone can publish an investment thesis, build a public strategy, and give others a way to discover their ideas. Feng turns investment research into something people can explore together."

**On-screen text:**

YOUR IDEA CAN GO BEYOND A POST.

---

## 1:43–2:00 — The Close

**Screen:** Cut to a clean end card with the Feng logo, product tagline, live application URL, and GitHub URL.

**Narration:**

"Feng is a social launchpad for tokenized investment strategies. We're turning investment theses into something people can discover, follow, and participate in. Don't just share what you believe. Put your thesis onchain. This is Feng."
